# v2.7.11 — Continue translation after page failures

## Translation queue

- Continue with later pages and queued chapters after a page exhausts its translation attempts, once its failed state has been saved (#112).
- Keep token-limit and context-length guidance visible without treating that advice as a reason to stop the entire queue.
- Retain failed pages for review and retry, and report an incomplete result even when later chapters finish successfully. Automatic inpainting still processes only successfully translated pages.
- Keep stopping on cancellation, non-retriable authentication/runtime errors, and failure-state persistence errors. This update does not increase automatic retry counts.

## Korean translation notation

- Relax the fixed-block translation prompt's Korean-only and Hangul-transliteration wording to request natural Korean with conventional notation.
- Clarify that Latin rank labels and Arabic numerals should remain in their usual form, such as `A랭크` and `1856년`, rather than being spelled out phonetically in Hangul. This is a prompt adjustment, not an additional model pass.

## Distribution and signing

Windows x64 and Apple Silicon macOS packages use the existing verified release workflows. The Windows installer is unsigned. The macOS DMG and ZIP are ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized; first launch may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
